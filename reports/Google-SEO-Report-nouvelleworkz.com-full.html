2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 82/100

What Works

  • All 118 XML sitemap URLs return clean HTTP 200 responses with zero 404 errors or redirection loops.
  • Canonical tags match Google index canonicals across crawled URLs.
  • Robots.txt is well-structured and explicitly allows major AI bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot).
  • HTTP/3 (QUIC) and Brotli compression active on LiteSpeed web server.

Findings

High Server Response Time (TTFB) in Field Data High

CrUX 28-day p75 TTFB is 2,646ms (classified as Poor by Google, >1,800ms). While static cached pages respond in ~16ms, dynamic requests or cache misses take 2.5s+ to generate in PHP/LiteSpeed.

Recommendation: Configure LiteSpeed Cache crawler to keep all sitemap pages warm, optimize database queries, and consider Cloudflare Enterprise/APO edge caching.

Missing HTTP Security Headers Medium

The server does not send Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS), X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, or Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy (COOP) headers.

Recommendation: Add Strict-Transport-Security, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and Referrer-Policy headers to the Apache/.htaccess or LiteSpeed configuration.

2.2 Content Quality & E-E-A-T

Score: 76/100

What Works

  • Exceptional E-E-A-T credentials: 96 of 119 pages cite HDB Licence HB-075810E, BCA, bizSAFE Level 3, and Director James Liang with 9+ years experience.
  • High-depth technical renovation pillar guides (HDB BTO schedules, Sintered stone vs quartz, F&B renovation SCDF/SFA licensing, Commercial reinstatement checklists) average 1,500+ words.
  • Factual tables with Singapore reference prices ($/sqft) establish high topical authority.

Findings

Vestigial WooCommerce Thin Content Pages Critical

Pages /shop/ (0 words), /cart/ (1 word), /checkout/ (1 word), and /my-account/ (0 words) are indexed and present in page-sitemap.xml.

Recommendation: Set noindex,follow on all WooCommerce utility pages and exclude them from page-sitemap.xml.

Thin Sub-Service Landing Pages Medium

Sub-service pages such as /residential/ceiling-partition/ (260 words), /residential/painting/ (212 words), and /residential/plastering/ (191 words) fall below the 300-word quality threshold.

Recommendation: Expand sub-service pages to 600-800 words with specific scopes, materials, pricing ranges, and case studies.

2.3 On-Page SEO

Score: 78/100

What Works

  • Descriptive keyword-rich URL slugs across all blog posts and main services.
  • Ranked for 1,000+ keywords in Google Search Console generating 26,747 impressions and 322 clicks over 28 days.
  • Comprehensive meta titles and meta descriptions present on core pages.

Findings

Multiple H1 Tags on Core Service Pages High

46 pages have heading hierarchy defects, including 2 H1 tags on key commercial and residential landing pages (e.g. /commercial/salon-retail-shop-renovation/, /residential/condo-renovation/).

Recommendation: Edit Elementor templates to ensure the main title is the sole <h1> and all sub-headings use <h2>, <h3>, or <h4>.

Orphaned & Low Internal Link Pages Medium

30 pages have 0 contextual internal links originating from other articles, limiting PageRank distribution.

Recommendation: Implement hub-and-spoke internal linking connecting specialized trade articles back to core service hubs.

2.4 Schema & Structured Data

Score: 88/100

What Works

  • Homepage features 3 JSON-LD blocks including consolidated LocalBusiness, GeneralContractor, HomeAndConstructionBusiness, and Person schema.
  • Valid AggregateRating (5.0 rating from 78 verified reviews) passes Google Rich Results inspection for review snippets.
  • Valid WebSite and WebPage schema with search action.

Findings

Deprecated FAQPage Rich Results Info

Google retired FAQPage rich results for all websites globally on May 7, 2026. While safe to retain for entity clarity, it no longer triggers SERP dropdowns.

Recommendation: Retain existing FAQPage schema without modification; do not invest development time in adding new FAQPage markup for SERP rich snippet expansion.

Missing BreadcrumbList Schema on Deep Pages Low

Sub-service and blog pages lack BreadcrumbList schema, missing an opportunity for clean breadcrumb display in search snippets.

Recommendation: Add automated BreadcrumbList JSON-LD to blog and service templates.

2.5 Performance & Core Web Vitals

Score: 62/100

What Works

  • Real user INP is under 100ms (CrUX field data - PASS).
  • Desktop First Contentful Paint is 0.4s and Desktop LCP is 0.5s in lab audits.

Findings

High Mobile Total Blocking Time (TBT 870ms) High

Mobile Lighthouse score is 41/100 primarily due to 870ms TBT and 4.5s lab LCP. Third-party Google Tag Manager scripts transfer 666KB and occupy 907ms of main-thread time.

Recommendation: Audit GTM containers to remove redundant tags, load GTM asynchronously with delay, and enable LiteSpeed JS deferral.

Field LCP Needs Improvement (2,999ms) Medium

Chrome UX Report 28-day p75 LCP is 2,999ms (Google Good threshold is 2,500ms). LCP image element render delay is compounded by TTFB latency.

Recommendation: Preload hero LCP image, add fetchpriority=high on above-the-fold banner, and optimize server TTFB.

6. Action Plan

Phase 1: Critical Fixes & Hygiene Week 1

  • Set noindex,follow on vestigial WooCommerce pages (/cart/, /checkout/, /my-account/, /shop/) and remove them from page-sitemap.xml.
  • Add missing security headers (HSTS, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, COOP) to .htaccess / LiteSpeed.
  • Correct dual <h1> tags on top commercial and residential service pages to enforce single H1 hierarchy.

Phase 2: Performance & Mobile Speed Optimization Weeks 2-3

  • Audit Google Tag Manager: remove redundant containers, defer non-critical tracking scripts to cut TBT by 400ms+.
  • Add fetchpriority=high and preload for hero LCP image on mobile homepage.
  • Configure LiteSpeed Cache crawler with scheduled warmup to eliminate TTFB spikes for first-time visitors.
  • Batch-update image alt text across top 100 service and portfolio images.

Phase 3: Content Expansion & Authority Month 2

  • Expand thin sub-service pages (/residential/ceiling-partition/, /painting/, /plastering/) to 600+ words with Singapore pricing and specs.
  • Deploy BreadcrumbList JSON-LD across all service and blog templates.
  • Build hub-and-spoke internal links from blog posts to core commercial & residential service hubs.
  • Add sameAs social & professional registry URLs to Director Person schema.

Phase 4: Monitoring, GEO Tracking & Iteration Ongoing

  • Monitor CrUX 28-day field LCP and TTFB trend toward passing green thresholds (<2,500ms and <800ms).
  • Track GSC high-impression quick wins (queries in positions 4-10 like nouvelle workz pte ltd reviews, l box ceiling, electrical rewiring cost).
  • Keep llms.txt updated with new 2026 pricing benchmarks and project completions.
  • Audit backlink profile monthly to monitor and disavow scrapers (e.g. kompromat100.info).
